How CampusRecover Works

1. Report

Report a lost or found item with details, location and time.

2. Match

Our system identifies possible matches between lost and found reports.

3. Verify

Submit ownership evidence and let the campus team verify the claim.

4. Recover

Collect your item through the authorized campus handover process.

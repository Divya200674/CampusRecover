Built around trust.

Finding an item is only the first step. CampusRecover focuses on making sure the item reaches the right person through a verification based claim process.

  • Private ownership evidence
  • Campus verification workflow
  • Organized claim tracking
  • Public listings without private proof

🔐 Campus Verification

A possible match does not automatically mean ownership. Claimants provide private evidence, while authorized campus staff can review the information before handover.
